What is ANNIVO and Why Does It Exist?

Traditional food delivery apps charge restaurants up to 35% commission, forcing them to inflate menu prices for delivery. ANNIVO changes the game with a fair, ethical, transparent model.

No inflated digital menus. Dishes on ANNIVO are priced exactly what you would pay if you walked into the restaurant in person.
